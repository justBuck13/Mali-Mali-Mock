FAQ

01. Drops & Releases

How do Limited Drops work? +

Our Limited Drops are produced in small quantities and never restocked. Once an item is marked as "Sold Out," it will not return to the store.

02. Orders & Payment

Can I cancel my order? +

Orders are processed immediately. Cancellations are only possible within 2 hours of purchase by contacting support.

03. Fit & Sizing

How do Mali Mali garments fit? +

Most of our silhouettes are designed with a "Boxy" or "Oversized" fit. We recommend taking your standard size for the intended look, or sizing down for a more classic fit.
