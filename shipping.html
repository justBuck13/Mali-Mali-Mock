Shipping & Returns

Domestic Shipping

All local orders are processed within **1-2 business days** and typically arrive within **5–10 business days**. During drop periods, please allow additional time for dispatch. Once shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email.

International Orders

Mali Mali ships globally. International rates are calculated at checkout. Please note that customs duties and taxes are the responsibility of the customer upon arrival in your country.

Returns & Exchanges

Items can be returned for an exchange or store credit within **7 days** of receipt (excluding intimates). Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original packaging. Final sale items (including limited drops and intimates) are not eligible for return.

Lost Packages

Mali Mali is not responsible for packages lost or stolen in transit. Please ensure your shipping address is secure and correct before finalizing your order.
