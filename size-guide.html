How to Measure

Chest/Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape measure parallel to the ground.

Length: Measure from the top of your shoulder (at the neckline) down to the desired hem length.

Sleeve: Measure from the center back neck, across the shoulder, down to your wrist.

Waist: Measure around your natural waistline, where your pants normally sit.

Hip: Measure around the fullest part of your hips, approximately 7-9 inches below your waistline.
