Privacy Policy

01. Collection of Information

We collect information from you when you register on our site, place an order, or subscribe to our newsletter. This includes your name, email address, shipping address, and payment details.

Any of the information we collect from you may be used to personalize your experience, improve our website, and process transactions. Your information, whether public or private, will not be sold, exchanged, or given to any other company without your consent.

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. All sensitive payment information is transmitted via Secure Socket Layer (SSL) technology and encrypted into our payment gateway providers' database.
