Return Policy

01. Returns

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase. Items must be in their original condition, unworn, with tags attached, and in the original packaging.

02. Exchanges

Exchanges are accepted within 30 days of purchase for the same item in a different size or color, subject to availability. If the exchange item is of a different value, you will be refunded or charged the difference.

03. Refunds

Refunds will be processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect your returned item. Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

04. Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item, please contact us immediately. We will arrange for a replacement or full refund at no cost to you.

05. Non-Returnable Items

Certain items are not eligible for return, including final sale items, personalized products, and items damaged due to misuse or normal wear.

06. How to Return

To initiate a return, contact our customer service team. We will provide a return authorization and shipping label. Returns without authorization may not be accepted.

07. Contact Us

For any questions about returns, please email us at returns@malimali.com or call our customer service at 1-800-MALI-MALI.
